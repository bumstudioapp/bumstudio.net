RPS Master 2.0 is the biggest update since launch — the game is now fully connected. Here's what
changed and why it matters.

## Your progress now lives in your account

Create a free account with email and password, or sign in with Google, and **everything follows
you**: profile, level, flowers, Conquest stages, IQ progress, inventory, titles and check-in
streaks. Log in on a new phone and your game is exactly where you left it. Logging out returns
the device to a clean guest profile.

Forgot your password? A 6-digit reset code by email gets you back in minutes — and you can delete
your account permanently from inside the app at any time.

## Friend Rooms

The Friends tab is now a private room system. Create a room, share its 6-character code, and play
a five-round match with zero ads and no effect on your win/loss record or reputation. Both
players vote on a rematch, so the rivalry can run as long as you like.

## Online Arena, refined

Arena matchmaking now prefers real players, keeps matches fast worldwide, and handles rematches
and disconnects far more gracefully. Your equipped hand icon and emotions now show on your
opponent's screen — and theirs on yours.

## Shop, flowers and fair extras

Version 2.0 opens the full economy: flower packs, the VIP crystal and diamond hand sets, emotions,
and a one-year ad-removal bundle that includes the exclusive gold hand set. Everything paid is
cosmetic — skill still decides every match.

## Under the hood

A new server backend (live at api.bumstudio.net) powers accounts, purchases verification and
online play, load-tested well beyond launch needs. Update notes from here on will land in this
Updates category — subscribe by RSS so you never miss a patch.
