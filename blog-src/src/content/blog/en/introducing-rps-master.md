Rock paper scissors is probably the first "game" most of us ever learned. **RPS Master** takes that
30-second playground classic and rebuilds it as a full mobile game — with ranked-feeling 1v1 matches,
a map to conquer, and reasons to come back every day.

## What is RPS Master?

RPS Master is a free game for Android and iOS developed by BumStudio. Every match is a best-of-five
duel of rock, paper and scissors — but around that simple core sits a complete game:

- **1v1 Arena** — face opponents from around the world in quick five-round matches.
- **Friend Rooms** — create a private room with a 6-character code and challenge a friend directly. No ads, ever, in friend rooms.
- **Conquest** — travel the Silk Road across 48 countries and 957 stages, beating the "ghost" move sequences left behind by players who cleared each stage before you.
- **Boost Your IQ** — short quiz questions between matches, with progress saved per question.
- **Titles & collection** — earn titles every five levels, collect hand icons and emotions, and build your profile.

## One currency, no pay-to-win

The whole economy runs on a single currency — the four-leaf lucky flower. You earn flowers by playing:
daily check-ins, arena wins, IQ answers and level-ups. Paid items in the shop are cosmetic only.
A prettier hand never beats a better read of your opponent.

## Where the game stands

RPS Master is now live on Google Play — version 2.0, with accounts, friend rooms and the full
shop, is the current release, and new content is in the works. Create a free account to keep
everything — your profile, Conquest progress, inventory and titles — safe when you change
phones, or just jump in as a guest and play right away.
