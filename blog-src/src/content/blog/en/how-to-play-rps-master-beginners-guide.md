Welcome to RPS Master! If you have ever thrown rock, paper or scissors, you already know 90% of the
game. This guide covers the remaining 10% — the modes, the currency and the little systems that
reward you for playing smart.

## The match: best of five rounds

Every match lasts **5 rounds**. In each round you pick rock, paper or scissors before the timer
runs out:

- Rock crushes scissors
- Scissors cut paper
- Paper covers rock

A draw scores nothing for either side. After round 5, the player with more round wins takes the
match — and after close losses, a rematch is one tap away.

## Start in Practice, then enter the Arena

**Practice** is your training ground: endless matches against the computer, where you can learn
an opponent's rhythm with nothing at stake. When you reach level 2, the **1v1 Arena** unlocks and
you start facing other players (or very convincing stand-ins while matchmaking is quiet).

Two things to watch in the Arena:

- **Winning a match earns you a lucky flower**, up to a daily cap.
- **Reputation matters.** Leaving an Arena match early costs reputation points; drop too low and
  the Arena locks until you earn it back by finishing practice matches.

## Flowers: the one and only currency

RPS Master has a single currency, the **four-leaf lucky flower**. You earn it from daily check-ins
(with a bonus every 7-day streak), Arena wins, daily match milestones, correct IQ answers and
level-ups. Spend it on hand icons and emotions in the shop — all cosmetic, never stronger.

## Conquest: beat the ghosts

In **Conquest** mode you clear stages across a map. Every player who passes a stage leaves a
"ghost" — the exact five-move sequence they used. To take the stage, you must beat that ghost.
Stars (1–3) decide your flower reward, and clearing a stage lets you leave a ghost of your own
for the next challenger.

## Your first session, step by step

1. Play 3 practice matches to learn the timing.
2. Check in from the Events tab for your daily flowers.
3. Try the IQ quiz — every first-time correct answer pays out.
4. Enter the Arena and finish every match you start (your reputation will thank you).

That's everything you need. The rest — titles, collections, friend rooms — unlocks naturally as
you level up. Good luck, and may your reads be sharp!
