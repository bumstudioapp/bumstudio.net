RPS Master launched on Google Play this week — and the next game is already on our bench.

**Slide Pocket 15** is our love letter to the classic plastic sliding puzzle: a vertical 3×5 board with 15 tiles, the sample picture right above it, and one spare empty slot at the top to slide tiles in and out. No timers screaming at you, no clutter — just you, the picture, and that one tile that refuses to go home.

Why a sliding puzzle? Because everybody over 25 has held one of these toys, and nobody ever forgot the feeling of finally completing the picture. We want that feeling in your pocket: quick sessions, one-thumb play, and pictures worth finishing.

The game is now in development. We will post dev notes here as it takes shape — the board, the art, and the difficulty curve we are tuning.

Until then, [play RPS Master on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.bumstudio.rpsgame) and sharpen your reflexes. The tiles are coming.
