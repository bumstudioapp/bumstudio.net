Inside the Game

One Simple Game, Four Ways to Play

Real players, real reads

Matchmaking pairs you with players worldwide — and smart stand-ins keep queues instant while the community grows.

Best of five, best of nerves

Five rounds per match: enough time to spot a habit, set a trap and punish it.

Reputation keeps it fair

Finish what you start. Leaving early costs reputation, and the Arena rewards players who play every match out.

Travel the Silk Road

Journey through 48 countries and 957 stages, from Vietnam all the way to Türkiye.

Beat the ghosts

Every cleared stage leaves a ghost — the exact five moves its conqueror used. Out-read the ghost to take the stage.

Stars pay flowers

Clear a stage with up to 3 stars to earn lucky flowers for hand icons and emotions.

A code, a friend, a match

Create a room, share its 6-character code, and play your rival directly — anytime.

Zero ads, zero pressure

Friend rooms never show ads and never touch your win/loss record or reputation.

Rematch by vote

Both players vote to run it back, so a good rivalry can last all evening.

Questions between matches

Short brain-training quizzes woven into your session, with progress saved per question.

First correct answer pays

Every IQ question pays out lucky flowers the first time you get it right.

Titles every five levels

Level up to earn titles, unlock the Arena at level 2 and grow your collection.

Our Process

From Idea to Pocket: How a BumStudio Game Is Made

Step 1 · Prototype

Feel first, features later

Every game starts as a tiny playable prototype. If the core feeling is not right in the first minute, we fix that before building anything else.

Step 2 · Build

Step 2 · Build

Flutter on the front, our own ASP.NET Core servers behind it — accounts, purchases and online play are built in-house and load-tested well beyond launch needs.

Step 3 · Launch

Launch, listen, update

We ship on Google Play, watch real player feedback, and update in the open — every patch note and dev story lands on our blog.
