Delete Your Account — RPS Master

You can delete your RPS Master account and all data associated with it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete from within the app

This is the fastest way — your account and data are erased immediately:

  1. Open RPS Master (version 2.0.0 or later) and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account below your account email.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete permanently.

What is deleted

Everything stored on our server under your account: your email address and password, profile (name, level, experience, in-game currency, reputation), conquest stage progress, quiz answers, owned and equipped items, check-in and lucky wheel state, and any ghosts you left in conquest stages. In-game currency and items have no monetary value and are deleted together with the account; they cannot be restored, transferred, or refunded.

If you play as a guest

Guest players have no account and nothing is stored on our server. Your progress lives only on your device and is erased when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

If you cannot access the app

Send an email from the address you registered with to bumstudio.app@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my RPS Master account”. We will verify the request and delete the account and its data within 30 days.
