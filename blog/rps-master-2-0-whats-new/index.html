RPS Master 2.0: Accounts, Friend Rooms and a Whole New Game

Version 2.0 connects RPS Master to the server: free accounts, cloud progress, friend rooms, Google sign-in, the new shop and more. Here's everything inside.

RPS Master 2.0 is the biggest update since launch — the game is now fully connected. Here’s what changed and why it matters.

Your progress now lives in your account

Create a free account with email and password, or sign in with Google, and everything follows you: profile, level, flowers, Conquest stages, IQ progress, inventory, titles and check-in streaks. Log in on a new phone and your game is exactly where you left it. Logging out returns the device to a clean guest profile.

Forgot your password? A 6-digit reset code by email gets you back in minutes — and you can delete your account permanently from inside the app at any time.

Friend Rooms

The Friends tab is now a private room system. Create a room, share its 6-character code, and play a five-round match with zero ads and no effect on your win/loss record or reputation. Both players vote on a rematch, so the rivalry can run as long as you like.

Online Arena, refined

Arena matchmaking now prefers real players, keeps matches fast worldwide, and handles rematches and disconnects far more gracefully. Your equipped hand icon and emotions now show on your opponent’s screen — and theirs on yours.

Shop, flowers and fair extras

Version 2.0 opens the full economy: flower packs, the VIP crystal and diamond hand sets, emotions, and a one-year ad-removal bundle that includes the exclusive gold hand set. Everything paid is cosmetic — skill still decides every match.

Under the hood

A new server backend (live at api.bumstudio.net) powers accounts, purchases verification and online play, load-tested well beyond launch needs. Update notes from here on will land in this Updates category — subscribe by RSS so you never miss a patch.
