Introducing RPS Master — the Rock Paper Scissors Game, Rebuilt for Your Phone

Meet RPS Master by BumStudio: 1v1 arenas, friend rooms, a Conquest map and brain-training quizzes in one free Android & iOS game.

Rock paper scissors is probably the first “game” most of us ever learned. RPS Master takes that 30-second playground classic and rebuilds it as a full mobile game — with ranked-feeling 1v1 matches, a map to conquer, and reasons to come back every day.

What is RPS Master?

RPS Master is a free game for Android and iOS developed by BumStudio. Every match is a best-of-five duel of rock, paper and scissors — but around that simple core sits a complete game:

  • 1v1 Arena — face opponents from around the world in quick five-round matches.
  • Friend Rooms — create a private room with a 6-character code and challenge a friend directly. No ads, ever, in friend rooms.
  • Conquest — travel a map of Asia province by province, beating the “ghost” move sequences left behind by players who cleared each stage before you.
  • Boost Your IQ — short quiz questions between matches, with progress saved per question.
  • Titles & collection — earn titles every five levels, collect hand icons and emotions, and build your profile.

One currency, no pay-to-win

The whole economy runs on a single currency — the four-leaf lucky flower. You earn flowers by playing: daily check-ins, arena wins, IQ answers and level-ups. Paid items in the shop are cosmetic only. A prettier hand never beats a better read of your opponent.

Where the game stands

RPS Master is currently in closed testing on Google Play, with its public release in preparation. Create a free account to keep everything — your profile, Conquest progress, inventory and titles — safe when you change phones, or just jump in as a guest and play right away.
