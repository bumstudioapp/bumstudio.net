How to Play RPS Master: a Beginner's Guide

New to RPS Master? Learn the five-round match format, the Arena, practice mode, flowers, reputation and Conquest in five minutes.

Welcome to RPS Master! If you have ever thrown rock, paper or scissors, you already know 90% of the game. This guide covers the remaining 10% — the modes, the currency and the little systems that reward you for playing smart.

The match: best of five rounds

Every match lasts 5 rounds. In each round you pick rock, paper or scissors before the timer runs out:

  • Rock crushes scissors
  • Scissors cut paper
  • Paper covers rock

A draw scores nothing for either side. After round 5, the player with more round wins takes the match — and after close losses, a rematch is one tap away.

Start in Practice, then enter the Arena

Practice is your training ground: endless matches against the computer, where you can learn an opponent’s rhythm with nothing at stake. When you reach level 3, the 1v1 Arena unlocks and you start facing other players (or very convincing stand-ins while matchmaking is quiet).

Two things to watch in the Arena:

  • Winning a match earns you a lucky flower, up to a daily cap.
  • Reputation matters. Leaving an Arena match early costs reputation points; drop too low and the Arena locks until you earn it back by finishing practice matches.

Flowers: the one and only currency

RPS Master has a single currency, the four-leaf lucky flower. You earn it from daily check-ins (with a bonus every 7-day streak), Arena wins, daily match milestones, correct IQ answers and level-ups. Spend it on hand icons and emotions in the shop — all cosmetic, never stronger.

Conquest: beat the ghosts

In Conquest mode you clear stages across a map. Every player who passes a stage leaves a “ghost” — the exact five-move sequence they used. To take the stage, you must beat that ghost. Stars (1–3) decide your flower reward, and clearing a stage lets you leave a ghost of your own for the next challenger.

Your first session, step by step

  1. Play 3 practice matches to learn the timing.
  2. Check in from the Events tab for your daily flowers.
  3. Try the IQ quiz — every first-time correct answer pays out.
  4. Enter the Arena and finish every match you start (your reputation will thank you).

That’s everything you need. The rest — titles, collections, friend rooms — unlocks naturally as you level up. Good luck, and may your reads be sharp!
