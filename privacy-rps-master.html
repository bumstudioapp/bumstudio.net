Privacy Policy — RPS Master

Effective date: November 2026 (applies to version 2.0.0 and later)

RPS Master is a rock-paper-scissors game developed by BumStudio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Playing as a guest

You can play RPS Master without an account. In guest mode no personal information is collected: your game progress is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

Information we collect when you register

If you create an account, we collect and store on our server:

  • Email address — used to identify your account and let you sign in on another device.
  • Password — stored only as a one-way cryptographic hash; we never store or see your plain-text password.
  • Profile information you set in the game — display name, avatar, and country.

You can also sign in with Google. In that case Google shares your email address and profile name with us so we can create your account or link it to an existing one; we never receive your Google password. If your account uses only Google sign-in, no password is stored on our side.

Password reset

If you forget your password, we email a 6-digit reset code to your registered email address. The code expires after 10 minutes.

Game data we store for registered accounts

To keep your progress available on any device you sign in on, we store your gameplay data: level and experience, in-game currency, reputation, match statistics, conquest stage progress, quiz answers, owned and equipped items, daily check-in and lucky wheel state, and the move sequences (“ghosts”) you leave in conquest stages.

Some of this data is visible to other players as part of the game: your display name, avatar, country, and level appear in online matches and next to your ghosts, together with the items you have equipped and any emotion stickers you send during a match.

How we use the information

We use the information only to operate the game: running your account, syncing progress between your devices, matchmaking for online matches, and keeping conquest ghosts available for other players. We do not sell your information, we do not use it for third-party marketing, and we do not send marketing emails.

In-app purchases

RPS Master offers optional in-app purchases, such as packs of lucky flowers and an emotions pack. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see or store your payment card details. Google Play shares the purchase record with us — which product was bought and a purchase token — so our server can verify the purchase and credit the items to your account. Purchase records are kept with your account and are removed when you delete your account.

Advertising

RPS Master shows ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, your approximate location (estimated from your IP address), information about how you interact with the app and with ads, and diagnostic data. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

We show two kinds of ads, both only outside friend and tournament rooms, which contain no ads:

  • Interstitial ads — a full-screen ad may appear after every three completed practice matches, and never more often than once every 90 seconds.
  • Rewarded ads — entirely optional. You can choose to watch an ad to earn 1 lucky flower, up to 5 times per day per account.

Ads are provided by Google under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not sell your information to advertisers.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated server data at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete account) or by following the instructions at bumstudio.net/delete-account-rps-master.html. Deletion is permanent. To confirm the request is really yours, the app asks for your account password — or, if your account uses only Google sign-in, asks you to sign in with Google again instead. Progress stored only on your device (guest mode) is removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Security

Communication between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as salted cryptographic hashes.

Children’s privacy

RPS Master is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has registered an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
