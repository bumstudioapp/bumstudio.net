Privacy Policy — RPS Master

Effective date: October 28, 2026

RPS Master is a rock-paper-scissors game developed by BumStudio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal data, and does not transmit personal data to us or to any server operated by us.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (level, in-game currency, unlocked items, settings, and similar game state) is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device, and deleting the app deletes this data.

Network connection

The app may check whether your device is connected to the internet in order to enable or disable online features. This check happens entirely on your device; no information about your connection is collected or sent to us.

Third-party services, ads, and analytics

The current version of RPS Master contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services that collect data. If a future version adds such features, we will update this policy and the app’s Google Play data safety information before those features are released.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from any user, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
